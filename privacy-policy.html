Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Chrysasynth is operated by Irwin Otian, France. For privacy requests, contact contact@chrysasynth.com.

2. Scope

This notice covers chrysasynth.com, Knowledge and forge.chrysasynth.com, including interactive audio tools, educational content and optional feedback features.

3. Information and purposes

Technical request information (such as IP address, browser and request logs) may be processed by hosting providers to deliver and secure the websites. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a reliable and secure service. If you contact us, we process your message and contact details to respond, based on our legitimate interest or steps requested before a contract, where applicable.

4. Browser storage and audio tools

Forge uses browser storage for preferences, consent choices, saved seeds and certain tool settings. Audio and image processing performed locally does not by itself require sending the source file to a server. Individual tools may have different behavior; review their instructions before uploading sensitive material. Strictly necessary storage supports requested functionality; optional tracking requires consent.

5. Optional usage analytics

The Forge implementation includes Google Analytics, loaded after acceptance of the usage insights prompt. Analytics may process device information, online identifiers and usage events to measure and improve the service. The legal basis is consent. You may refuse optional analytics or withdraw consent at any time by clearing the site’s stored consent preference in your browser and refusing the prompt when it reappears, or by contacting us for assistance. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing. The shared consent preference is designed to last up to 180 days.

6. Feedback, ratings and verification

When enabled, Forge feedback and ratings send your message, tool identifier, rating and a browser-generated voter identifier to a Cloudflare Worker and database. Cloudflare Turnstile verifies submissions and may receive technical information including IP address. A pseudonymous IP-derived identifier is used for rate limiting. These functions support service improvement and abuse prevention on the basis of legitimate interests. Please avoid entering sensitive information in feedback.

7. Recipients and international transfers

Depending on the feature used, recipients may include Cloudflare, GitHub, Google (analytics, fonts or email) and content delivery providers. External links, including YouTube, lead to services governed by their own privacy notices. Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, applicable transfer safeguards must be used.

8. Retention

Technical records, feedback and correspondence are retained according to their purpose, including service security, responding to requests and applicable legal obligations. You may contact us for information about the retention applicable to your data.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing. You may withdraw consent at any time. You can lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, CNIL. Contact the controller using the address above. Providing optional data is voluntary; without it the associated optional feature may not work.

10. Changes

We may update this notice when features or processing practices change. Material changes will be communicated as appropriate.
